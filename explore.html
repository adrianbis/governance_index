The frameworks in index space

Each framework sits at its three axis averages. Turn the cube to see all three axes at once, or flatten it to any two.

Reading the map

The floor of the cube is power concentration against reversibility; the height is efficiency. The faint dots on the back walls are each framework's shadow: where it sits on that pair of axes alone.

The three flat views are exact two-axis charts. In the turning view, perspective makes positions approximate, so read exact numbers from the flat views, the side panel or the Scores page.

“Closest” and “farthest” in the side panel are straight-line distances across all three axes, with each axis weighted equally. The map is drawn from the Scores table each time the page loads, so the two cannot disagree.
